# v90d/v34: V.34 primary channel, bits <-> 2D points

Everything between "bits" and "a sequence of 2D constellation points at the
symbol rate" for V.34 modulation, which V.90 uses unchanged for the upstream
direction (analogue modem transmits, the server receives). No filters, no
carrier, no timing: those live elsewhere.

Written from ITU-T V.34 (02/98) clauses 7, 8, 9 and 10.1.3
(`../refs/itu/T-REC-V.34-199802-I.txt` and the PDF for figures), plus V.90
for what it changes. `../refs/spandsp` was never opened while writing this
code; nothing here derives from any other implementation.

## Scaling convention

A `v34_pt_t` is a complex number, `int16_t` real and imaginary parts, in Q7
"constellation units". The grid of Figure 5 has odd-integer coordinates, so
point 0 = (1, 1) is `{128, 128}`. Q7 is exact for the precoder, which works
in multiples of 2^-7.

* Encoder output `x(n)` and decoder input `xhat(n)`: Q7 units. The decoder
  expects its input scaled so that a noiseless point lands exactly on the
  transmitted value: the gain control in front of it must deliver that.
* Mean energies in units^2: S, S-bar and all 4-point signals: 2; 16-point
  signals: 10; data mode: `v34_params_t.energy_q8 / 256` (nominal, from the
  shell mapper ring statistics, uniform data; measured values agree within
  0.8 % at 3000 and 3200 sym/s). Precoding changes the energy of x(n)
  slightly; that is not included.
* `v34_gain_q14(energy_q8)` is the Q14 factor that brings a signal of that
  energy to the power of the 4-point signals. Whether the modulator uses it
  is its business; the encoder always emits unscaled units.
* PP is a unit-magnitude phasor returned in Q14 (`v34_pp_point`).
* Precoder coefficients `h[k] = {re, im}` are in the 2.14 format of the MP
  sequence. All zero means no precoding.
* Bits: `get()` returns one data bit, `put()` receives one. First bit in
  time first. In packed arguments (`v34_tmod_put`, `v34_shell_map`) bit 0 is
  the first bit in time.

## The shared parameter struct

`v34_cfg_t` holds what the pair must agree on: symbol rate, data rate,
trellis (16/32/64 states), shaping (minimum/expanded), nonlinear parameter,
scrambler polynomial of the transmitter, precoder coefficients.
`v34_params_init()` expands it into a `v34_params_t` (framing J, P, N, b, r;
mapping K, q, M, L, w; shell mapper tables; constellation bitmap; nominal
energy). One `v34_params_t` is shared read-only by any number of encoders
and decoders.

Constellation scaling is not a field: points are always in units and the
energy is reported, see above.

## How the decoder works

Per received symbol (`v34_rx_put`):

1. Whitening: `r(n) = xhat(n) + sum h(k) xhat(n-k)`. If xhat is x plus the
   noise the coefficients were designed for, r is `y(n) = u(n) + c(n)` plus
   white noise. y lies on the odd-integer grid whatever the coefficients
   are, and the trellis code is defined on y.
2. Subset slicing: nearest valid point and squared distance for each 2D
   subset of Figure 9 (8 subsets; the 16-state code only distinguishes 4
   cosets, so it slices 4). Without precoding "valid" means inside the
   L-point constellation (exact nearest point, also outside the boundary).
   With precoding y is not confined to the constellation, so any grid point
   up to `ymax` is valid.
3. Every second symbol: one Viterbi step on the 4D code. A branch leaving
   state S with inputs Y1..Y4 is labelled by those bits and by the parity
   `Y0(S) ^ V0`; its metric is the best pair of 2D subsets with that label
   (Table 13 and the parity `lsb(s1) ^ lsb(s2)`). Survivor history is a
   ring of `V34_VIT_DEPTH` (24) 4D symbols; each step traces back from the
   best state and releases the oldest 4D symbol.
4. Decided y points go through the inverse precoder (the encoder's own
   recursion: rebuild p(n) and c(n) from decided x, `u = y - c`,
   `x = y - p`), inverse mapper, differential decoder, and once per mapping
   frame the inverse shell mapper, inverse parser and descrambler.

Decision delay: 24 4D symbols = 48 symbols (15 ms at 3200) plus up to one
mapping frame. Depth 16 gave the same BER in an experiment; 24 is margin.

Tentative decisions (`tent` argument, zero delay): without precoding, the
nearest constellation point. With precoding, the nearest y-grid point to
r(n) minus p(n) rebuilt from the previous tentative decisions, so it is an
estimate of x(n) in the same domain as the input. If that loop drifts more
than 1.5 units from the input it is restarted from the input.

The inverse precoder on the decision path compares the x it rebuilds with
the x it received (delayed) and restarts from the received value if they
differ by more than 6 units in a coordinate (`reseeds` counter). That only
catches runaway. With minimum-phase coefficients an error dies out by
itself; with coefficients that are not minimum phase the recursion is
exact in a clean channel (tested, 1 - 1.5 D) but a decision error can ring
until the guard trips.

## Spec ambiguities and the reading chosen

1. **S and S-bar (10.1.3.7).** The text extraction loses the overbar, so the
   clause appears to define S twice. From the PDF: S alternates point 0 and
   point 0 rotated 90 degrees counterclockwise and ends on the rotated
   point; S-bar alternates point 0 rotated 180 and 270 degrees and begins on
   the 180 degree point. `v34_s_point(i, len)` is aligned to the end of the
   segment for that reason.
2. **"Rotated counterclockwise" versus the mapper's clockwise.** 9.6.1
   rotates clockwise, 10.1.3.7 counterclockwise. Both taken literally.
3. **PP amplitude (10.1.3.6).** The formula gives a unit-magnitude phasor
   and no relation to the other signals' power. Returned as Q14 unit
   magnitude; the caller scales.
4. **Data mode power.** The Recommendation says only that the transmitter
   should compensate so that power stays the same. The library reports the
   nominal energy and leaves scaling to the caller.
5. **TRN to J-type transition (10.1.3.3, 10.1.3.9).** "The differential
   encoder shall be initialized using the final symbol of the transmitted
   TRN sequence", but TRN is not differentially encoded and the scrambler
   is not mentioned. Chosen: the differential encoder state becomes the
   rotation (quadrant) of the last TRN symbol (`v34_tmod_trn` keeps `z` up
   to date) and the scrambler runs on without reset. V.90 words its own
   signals separately: SCR needs no initialisation of either, CP sequences
   start with both at zero (`v34_tmod_init`).
6. **B1 bit inversions (10.1.3.1).** "As if the data frame were the last
   data frame in a superframe": B1 uses half-frame positions 2J-2 and 2J-1
   of Table 12 and the first data frame after B1 starts a new superframe at
   position 0.
7. **Table 12 bit order.** The pattern is read left to right, the leftmost
   bit applying to the first half data frame of the superframe. Patterns
   0x1DFE (14 bits, J = 7) and 0x77FA (16 bits, J = 8).
8. **Figure 9 outside the 16 points drawn.** The figure labels 16 points.
   Read as: the eight subsets are the cosets of the lattice
   {(4i, 4j), i + j even}, so the labelling has period 8 in each
   coordinate. With that reading Table 13 has the structure the code
   requires (checked in `test_constel.c`), which is strong evidence.
9. **Figure 10 (16-state encoder) input labels.** The inputs between the
   delay elements read Y2, Y2, Y1 after delays 1, 2, 3 with Y0 fed back to
   the first delay and the first adder. The figure is small; the reading
   passes the rotational invariance check of item 10.
10. **Figures 11 and 12 (32 and 64 states).** Same problem, worse for
    Figure 12 with its two AND gates. Readings are in `v34_core.c`. The
    check for all three codes (`test_trellis.c`): an encoded sequence
    turned by 90 degrees is again a code sequence, from exactly one
    starting state, and a sequence with one point moved is not, from any
    state. A misread connection would very probably break that, but it is
    the only check there is: no comparison with an independent
    implementation was made.
11. **"2w" in 9.6.2.** The quantiser step printed as "2w" is 2^w (w = 1 for
    b < 56, else 2): the text extraction flattens superscripts, the same
    paragraph prints 2^-7 as "2-7".
12. **Rounding in the precoder.** p(n) is rounded to 2^-7 and c(n) to 2^w,
    ties in both cases towards zero (smaller magnitude), each coordinate
    separately, as 9.6.2 says. Encoder and decoder share one function.
13. **M for expanded shaping (9.2).** The formula can land on x.5; rounding
    up reproduces every row of Table 10.
14. **Parser for b <= 12 (9.3.2).** With 8 to 12 bits per mapping frame
    there is no shell mapping. Each of the four 4D symbols gets I1 and I2;
    I3 exists in the first n - 8 symbols (n = bits in this mapping frame),
    else 0.
15. **Shell mapper "largest B / C / D such that" (9.4).** The search is
    restricted to the valid index range of each table, which the text
    leaves implicit.
16. **Rates with an auxiliary channel.** `v34_map_params` accepts any total
    rate of Table 8 (multiples of 200 offsets included) so that the tables
    can be checked. `v34_params_init` only accepts plain data rates.
17. **Scrambler polynomials.** V.34 assigns GPC to the call modem and GPA
    to the answer modem. V.90 fixes them by role instead: the analogue
    modem (the transmitter this decoder listens to) uses GPA, the digital
    modem GPC. `cfg.scrambler` names the polynomial of the transmitting
    side.
18. **What the receiver does about precoding.** Not specified at all. The
    whitening filter plus recursive inverse precoder described above is the
    textbook receiver for this precoder; the guards and the tentative
    decision under precoding are choices of this implementation.

Independent checks of the readings: Figure 5 from the ordering rule equals
the figure parsed from the PDF (all 416 points); Tables 8 and 10 from the
formulas equal the printed tables (all 130 rows); the three convolutional
encoders are rotationally invariant; the encoder output satisfies the
decoder-independent syndrome check in `test_trellis.c`.

## Performance

White Gaussian noise added to the points, Es/N0 = mean constellation energy
over 2 sigma^2, 10^6 bits per point (`test_ber.c`, numbers from
`make test`; the ARM run uses 60000 bits and is correspondingly rougher).
"Slicer" is the same receiver with the trellis decoder bypassed
(`v34_rx_set_hard`), deciding each symbol on its own.

| configuration | BER 1e-3, slicer | BER 1e-3, Viterbi | gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3200 / 19200, 16 states, minimum shaping | 25.90 dB | 21.14 dB | 4.76 dB |
| 3200 / 28800, 16 states | 34.98 dB | 30.36 dB | 4.63 dB |
| 3000 / 9600, 16 states | 17.38 dB | 12.63 dB | 4.75 dB |
| 3200 / 19200, 16 states, expanded shaping | 25.76 dB | 20.97 dB | 4.79 dB |
| 3200 / 19200, 32 states | 25.96 dB | 21.10 dB | 4.86 dB |
| 3200 / 19200, 64 states | 25.99 dB | 21.15 dB | 4.84 dB |

At 1e-4 the test prints gains of 4.8 to 5.8 dB, from very few error
events: take them as "about 5 dB". Two things to keep in mind when reading
the gain. It is measured against slicing the same signal, whose
constellation carries the code's redundant bit; against an uncoded system
of the same rate and power it would be about 1.5 dB less. And BER here
includes the error multiplication of the shell mapper and descrambler
(one wrong point costs tens of bit errors: 33 for a single garbage symbol
in `test_resync.c`), for both curves.

With precoding (`test_precoder.c`, 3200 / 19200, 16 states, 1.5 * 10^6
bits per point): noise that is white after the whitening filter gives the
same BER as no precoding at the same level (22 dB: 5.4e-5 without, 3.7e-5
to 6.3e-5 with two coefficient sets), fed either as y directly or as x
with the correspondingly coloured noise. Clean round trips are error free
for five coefficient sets including a non-minimum-phase one.

## Resynchronisation

The decoder has no alignment search. It must be started on the first
symbol of B1 and it then counts symbols. What happens when things go
wrong (`test_resync.c`, numbers from `make test`):

* **Error bursts** (garbage symbols, alignment kept): self-recovering. The
  trellis, the differential decoder, the shell mapper frame and the
  self-synchronising descrambler all flush. Error-free again 0 to 12
  symbols after the end of the burst without precoding, 10 to 48 symbols
  with a strong precoder.
* **Odd number of symbols lost or inserted** (4D symbol alignment lost):
  no recovery, BER 0.5. Detectable: `v34_rx_quality()` rises from the noise
  variance to about 0.43 units^2 (value 110 and above; the test requires
  more than 0.35 units^2 and 2.5 times the previous value).
* **Even number, not a multiple of 8** (mapping frame alignment lost): no
  recovery, BER about 0.5. The quality metric hardly moves because the 4D
  symbols are still aligned; `shell_errors` does (about 10 % of the mapping
  frames at 19200) when K > 0. Exception: at 4800 bit/s every 4D symbol
  carries its bits alone and a 2-symbol slip costs only 0.4 % BER.
* **Multiple of 8 but not of the data frame** (mapping frame switching and
  bit inversion out of step): persistent BER of a few per cent to 16 %.
  Visible in `shell_errors` when the rate has high and low frames.
* **A whole data frame**: only the superframe bit inversion is out of step:
  BER about 2 %, 18 bad shell frames in 5000.
* 4800 bit/s at 3200 has no shell mapper (K = 0), so `shell_errors` stays
  0 there; only the quality metric is available.

So: the caller should watch `v34_rx_quality()` (alarm at about 0.35
units^2, or better relative to its settled value) and the rate of
`shell_errors` per `frames`, and recover the way V.34 does, by a retrain or
rate renegotiation, which ends with a new B1 and a new `v34_rx_init()`.
`b1_errors` out of `b1_bits` tells whether the start was right. There is no
superframe (bit inversion) search in this library.

## Cost

Integer only. Per symbol the decoder does no division (one small modulo
per half data frame when J = 7) and, without precoding, no 64-bit
arithmetic. With precoding it adds three complex products accumulated in
64 bits for the whitening filter and three for each of the two inverse
precoders (tentative and decided).

State: `sizeof(v34_rx_t)` is about 7.5 KB (survivor history of 24 steps is
4 KB of it), `v34_params_t` 1040 bytes, `v34_tx_t` about 150 bytes. No
allocation, nothing global but constant tables (about 2 KB).

Executed ARM instructions per 2D symbol, ARM926 build with the project's
cross compiler at -O2, counted by qemu (`make cost-arm`), steady state,
noise about 2 to 4 dB better than the decoder's 1e-3 point:

| configuration | encoder | decoder | decoder + tentative decision | decoder in rfir MACs | share of the box at 3200 sym/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3200 / 4800, 16 states | 326 | 1706 | 1770 | 279 | 3.5 % |
| 3200 / 19200, 16 states | 659 | 1869 | 1933 | 306 | 3.8 % |
| 3200 / 31200, 16 states | 778 | 1923 | 1987 | 315 | 3.9 % |
| 3200 / 19200, 16 states, precoded | 789 | 2249 | 2265 | 368 | 4.6 % |
| 3200 / 19200, 32 states | 658 | 5303 | 5368 | 869 | 10.9 % |
| 3200 / 19200, 64 states | 665 | 11174 | 11238 | 1831 | 23.0 % |

The last two columns are an estimate, not a measurement. The reference is
the real FIR kernel of `../stage0/bench.c`, which ran at 25.4 MMAC/s on the
actual 400 MHz ARM926 and takes 6.10 instructions per MAC in the same qemu
count. "Share" assumes the decoder needs as many cycles per instruction as
that loop. It has more branches and byte table lookups, so the true figure
is probably somewhat higher; nothing was timed on the box. For comparison,
stage0's budget model allows the "trellis etc." block 30 % of the filter
work, which is about 4 % of the core.

Where the 1869 instructions of the 19200 case go: trellis step (16 branch
metrics, 64 add-compare-selects, normalisation) about 950, slicing the four
cosets about 340, traceback 114, the rest is the back end (inverse mapper,
shell unmapper, parser, descrambler, one bit at a time). The tentative
decision costs 64 instructions more; under precoding its loop always runs,
so asking for it is nearly free there.

`make cost-arm` fails if a 16-state configuration exceeds `COST_LIMIT`
(3000) instructions per symbol. `make test` and `make test-arm` only print
wall time (meaningless under qemu) and, on the host, check a loose bound.

A server that dictates the trellis in its MP should ask for 16 states: the
32 and 64-state decoders use the generic 8-subset path and were not tuned.

## Not implemented, not verified

* **Nonlinear encoder (9.7).** Implemented (`v34_nl.c`, `test/test_nl.c`):
  `v34_tx_symbol` applies it when `cfg.nonlinear` is set, and a receiver
  calls `v34_nl_decode` on its equalised point before `v34_rx_put`. Reading
  chosen: the "average energy of x(n)" in (9-35) is the nominal mean energy
  of the data constellation (`energy_q8`). The encoder raises the mean power
  (0.64 dB at 3200 / 28800) and the transmitter gives that back:
  `energy_tx_q8` is the mean energy of the encoded signal, for
  `v34_gain_q14`. Checked against a Conexant CX93001 on the box
  (2026-11-15): its encoded signal decodes with that scale (best fit 0.92,
  ours 0.93) and with this energy reference, and it decodes ours.
* **Auxiliary channel.** Not implemented (framing parameters only).
* **MP, CP, INFO, E sequence contents.** Only the modulation (J-type
  modulator and demodulator); the bit formats and CRCs are not here.
* **Detection of S, S-bar, PP.** Not here by design.
* **Superframe or frame resynchronisation.** None, see above.
* **Interoperability.** Nothing was tested against an independent V.34
  implementation or a real modem. The encoder and decoder agree with each
  other and with hand-derived vectors for the shell mapper, mapper,
  precoder rounding and one trellis encoder sequence; the readings of
  Figures 9 to 12 rest on structural checks. A wrong reading shared by
  encoder and decoder and consistent with those checks would not be seen.
* **32 and 64 states.** Encode, decode, round trip and BER tested. In the
  BER test they gain nothing measurable over 16 states at 1e-3 (4.86 and
  4.84 dB against 4.76 dB; the runs are too short to resolve the few
  tenths of a dB expected). Decoder cost is 2.8 and 6 times the 16-state
  decoder and nothing was done to reduce it.
* **Cost on the box.** Instruction counts come from qemu, the CPU share is
  an estimate; nothing was timed on the DVF9918 itself.
* **Noise in the precoded tests** is synthetic: white on y, or shaped by
  1/H on x. No real channel, no equaliser in the loop.
* **Test order.** The unit tests for scrambler, parameters, constellation,
  shell mapper, trellis encoder and training signals were written before
  the code they test. The round trip, precoder, BER and resync tests were
  written after a first decoder existed; to make up for that, ten
  deliberate bugs were injected in a scratch copy and each was caught by
  those tests.
